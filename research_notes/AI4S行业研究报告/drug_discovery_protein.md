## Q3. Business models: SaaS/software, AI CRO, milestone partnerships, own pipeline. Which work economically?

### Takeaway
Pure software is a steady but slow, modest-margin business (Schrödinger's ACV is about $220M after 30 years). Partnership deals carry low upfronts and large biobucks. The models that produced profits in 2026 are asset out-licensing and NewCo deals (Insilico in 1H26, XtalPi in 2025), which are lumpy. Platform-only companies are the best funded privately, but value ultimately depends on clinical assets.

### Cited Findings
- **Software/SaaS:** Schrödinger guides FY26 ACV of $218–228M (+10–15%). Software revenue fell 10% in Q2'26 during the hosted transition, and software gross margin fell to 71% from 76%. The company relies on drug discovery milestones (guidance raised to $65–75M) and one-off gains (Ajax/Lilly) for profitability — [Panabee](https://www.panabee.com/news/schrodinger-earnings-q2-2026-report); [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9009674/schrodinger-inc-sdgr-q2-2026-earnings-call-highlights-acv-surges-27-on-bunsen-launch-and-strong-drug-discovery-milestones)
- **Software is marginal at Insilico:** software revenue was only $2.70M of $106.3M in 1H26. Pipeline and BD revenue was $103.1M — [新华财经](https://www.cnfin.com/gs-lb/detail/20260831/4462734_1.html)
- **AI CRO + automation (XtalPi):** drug discovery solutions revenue was RMB 200.1M in 1H26. The rest came from its intelligent automation segment (which includes chemicals and robotics) — [凤凰财经](https://finance.ifeng.com/c/8vk8lSWaaSA)
- **Low-upfront, high-milestone structure:**
  - DoveTree–XtalPi: $51M upfront on a $5.99B deal (under 1%).
  - Lilly–Insilico (Mar 2026): $115M upfront plus up to $2.63B in milestones and royalties.
  - Isomorphic–Lilly: $45M upfront plus $1.7B. Isomorphic–Novartis: $37.5M upfront plus $1.2B.
  - Analysts describe "low upfront + high milestone" as the standard risk pricing for AI drug deals.
  - Sources: [Tiger Brokers](https://www.itiger.com/news/2639007846); [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/lilly-insilico-ai-drug-discovery-deal); [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/lilly-novartis-sign-ai-partnership-with-alphabet-s-isomorphic)
- **Own pipeline is costly:** Recursion's cash burn is guided below $390M for 2026, its TTM loss was about $716M against TTM revenue of about $44M (to Sept 2025), and it has had to cut programs and staff — [Kalkine](https://kalkine.ca/news/stocks/ai-specialist-recursion-trims-pipeline-in-latest-shakeup); [Fierce](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/recursion-lays-20-staff-wake-pipeline-cutbacks)
- **Open-model "picks and shovels":** Boltz PBC (seed $28M) keeps its models open, does not develop drugs, and monetizes through enterprise partners such as Pfizer — [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/open-source-ai-drug-discovery-boltz-pfizer)

### Inferences
- The "hybrid" model works best financially: own a pipeline for optionality, then out-license early to MNCs to fund the platform. Insilico's 1H26 profit is the strongest data point. However, recognized revenue depends on a few deals and may not recur.
- Pure SaaS faces pricing pressure from open-source models (Boltz-2, OpenFold3). This partly explains Schrödinger's slow software growth.
- Headline "deal values" (billions) overstate the economics. Upfronts are typically 1–5% of the headline figure.

## Q5. Biology foundation models: AlphaFold3, ESM3, Evo 2, Boltz-2, open vs closed; market size

### Takeaway
Open models have caught up on structure prediction. Boltz-2 (MIT license, with binding affinity), OpenFold3 (consortium with Novo Nordisk) and Evo 2 (Apache 2.0, up to 40B parameters) are fully open. AlphaFold3 remains restricted, with commercial use channelled through Isomorphic. Third-party estimates of "AI in drug discovery" market size vary widely, at about $1.7–6.9B in 2024–25.

### Cited Findings
- **Boltz-2:** released June 2025 by MIT CSAIL / Jameel Clinic and Recursion. It predicts structure and binding affinity, and the model, weights and training pipeline are under the MIT license. Boltz PBC was founded Jan 2026 (London, $28M seed) to keep the models open. Pfizer collaborates on Boltz-2 and BoltzGen — [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/mit-and-recursion-unveil-boltz-2-revolutionary-open-source-biomolecular-co-folding-model); [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/open-source-ai-drug-discovery-boltz-pfizer)
- **OpenFold3:** a fully open alternative to AF3 from a consortium including Columbia, LLNL and Novo Nordisk — [Scaling Biotech](https://scalingbiotech.substack.com/p/open-source-protein-models-openfold)
- **Evo 2 (Arc Institute + NVIDIA, Feb 2025):** a DNA language model across all domains of life. ### Inferences
- Structure prediction is commoditizing. Competitive advantage is moving to:
  - proprietary wet-lab data generation (Recursion phenomics, XtalPi robotics, Lila autonomous labs);
  - affinity and generative design (Boltz-2, BoltzGen, Chai-2, ESM3);
  - clinical assets.
- A better proxy for the "real" market than vendor TAM reports is deal flow: the 2026 private rounds of Isomorphic ($2.1B), Helixon ($787M), Chai ($400M) and Generate's IPO ($400M) exceed the stated annual market sizes.
